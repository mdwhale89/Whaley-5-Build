The CD Projekt Red Trilogy

Carving an independent interactive legacy with the author's permission to diverge from canonical book continuity.

Narrative Freedom & Creative Departure

When CD Projekt Red approached Andrzej Sapkowski to acquire video game adaptation rights, the book series was widely viewed as concluded with The Lady of the Lake. Because there was no confirmation of ongoing novels at the time, the studio secured rights to forge a continuation rather than a direct beat-for-beat adaptation.

By picking up Geralt's journey after amnesia resets his immediate past, the developers respected the tone and lore while having the latitude to introduce fresh conflicts, reinvent Nilfgaardian politics, and craft original conclusions for fan-favorite characters.

Key Innovations of the Interactive Medium

  • Delayed Consequences: Decisions made during quests often do not reveal their moral fallout until hours later in the campaign.
  • Alchemy & Preparation: Translating witcher lore into gameplay through decoctions, oils, signs, and bestiary research.
  • Player-Driven Morality: Eliminating binary paragon/renegade choices in favor of competing lesser evils.
