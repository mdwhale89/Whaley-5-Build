# The Witcher Archive

A responsive, multi-page web resource exploring Andrzej Sapkowski's dark fantasy universe across literature, video games, comic books, and television.

## Included Pages
* **Overview (`index.html`):** The Conjunction of the Spheres, the creation of witchers, the Witcher's Creed, and core franchise portals.
* **Books & Comics (`screenings.html`):** Andrzej Sapkowski's 9-book literary canon (including *Crossroads of Ravens*), recommended and chronological reading orders, Dark Horse Comics arcs, and Omnibus collections.
* **The Games (`workshops.html`):** CD Projekt Red's video game trilogy, supported platforms, Ciri's three divergent endings in *The Witcher 3*, the 2027 *Songs of the Past* expansion, and *The Witcher 4* (Project Polaris).
* **TV Series (`collaborations.html`):** The Netflix live-action adaptation, analyzing fidelity versus progressive script divergences and the Geralt recasting.
