The CD Projekt Red Trilogy

An interactive evolution of The Continent: tracing Geralt's journey from amnesiac slayer to passing the torch to Ciri.

Narrative Freedom & Creative Departure

When CD Projekt Red approached Andrzej Sapkowski to acquire video game adaptation rights, the book saga was widely treated as concluded with The Lady of the Lake. Because there was no confirmation of ongoing novels at the time, the studio secured rights to forge a continuation rather than a direct beat-for-beat adaptation.

By picking up Geralt's journey after amnesia resets his immediate past, the developers respected the established tone and lore while having the latitude to introduce fresh conflicts, reinvent Nilfgaardian politics, and craft original conclusions for fan-favorite characters.

The Trilogy: Evolution of the Legend

The Witcher (2007)

Available on: PC (Microsoft Windows, macOS)

The saga opens with Geralt found near death in the forests outside Kaer Morhen, stripped of his memories. Centered around Vizima and the outskirts of Temeria, Geralt investigates the criminal syndicate Salamandra while contending with rising tensions between the racist Order of the Flaming Rose and the non-human guerrilla Scoia'tael.

The Witcher 2: Assassins of Kings (2011)

Available on: PC (Windows, macOS, Linux), Xbox 360 (Backward compatible on Xbox One & Xbox Series X/S)

Framed for the murder of King Foltest, Geralt embarks on a high-stakes political pursuit across the Pontar Valley to hunt down the rogue witcher Letho of Gulet. The title introduced branching mid-game storylines that split player perspective between Iorveth's rebels and Vernon Roche's Special Forces, exposing Nilfgaardian conspiracies destabilizing the Northern Realms.

The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt & Ciri's Three Destinies

Available on: PC, PlayStation 5, PlayStation 4, Xbox Series X/S, Xbox One, Nintendo Switch

Widely regarded as the benchmark of narrative open-world RPGs, The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt shifts the lens from regional crown wars to an urgent personal quest: locating Geralt’s adopted daughter, Cirilla Fiona Elen Riannon. Pursued relentlessly by Eredin and the spectral cavalry of the Wild Hunt for her Elder Blood, Ciri's survival and self-confidence hinge entirely on key parental choices made by Geralt.

Depending on whether Geralt empowered or stifled her autonomy, the saga branches into three distinct endings:

  • The Witcheress (The Heroic Path): If Geralt supports Ciri's independence and avoids taking her to Emperor Emhyr, Ciri stops the White Frost and survives. Geralt lies to the Emperor claiming she died, later meeting her at the White Orchard inn to gift her a custom silver sword, Zireael, as she embarks on the open road as a professional witcher.
  • The Empress (The Duty Path): If Geralt takes Ciri to meet her biological father in Nilfgaard and the empire wins the war, Ciri realizes she can protect far more lives by governing. After stopping the White Frost, she tearfully bids farewell to Geralt to assume the Nilfgaardian throne, choosing statecraft over the Witcher's path.
  • The Tragic Fate (The Worst Ending): If Geralt makes overly protective, dismissive, or controlling choices (such as accepting Emhyr's money or preventing her from expressing grief), Ciri lacks the self-belief required to survive her confrontation with the White Frost and never returns. A grief-stricken Geralt travels to the Crookback Bog to slaughter the last Crone, Weavess, retrieving Ciri's stolen medallion before collapsing in a monster-swarmed hut, resigned to death.

From Complete Edition to "Songs of the Past" (2027)

While most modern players experience the saga through The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt – Complete Edition—which integrated the acclaimed expansions Hearts of Stone and Blood and Wine alongside next-gen graphical overhauls—Geralt's journey is not over yet. A brand-new DLC expansion titled Songs of the Past is scheduled for release in 2027, delivering an unexpected return to the Continent with new contracts, folklore, and narrative arcs prior to the launch of the next generation.

The Next Era: The Witcher 4 (Project Polaris)

Target Platforms: PC, PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X/S

With Geralt of Rivia’s mainline trilogy concluding and transitioning through the upcoming expansions, CD Projekt Red is ushering in a brand-new saga built on Unreal Engine 5. Rather than continuing with Geralt as the protagonist, the new chapter positions Ciri to step into the central protagonist role—navigating a shifting Continent under a new Witcher school.
