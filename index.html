The Conjunction of the Spheres

Over a millennium ago, a cataclysmic cosmic collision known as the Conjunction of the Spheres forcibly trapped different dimensions together. Chaotic magical energy—Chaos—bled into the world, along with otherworldly beasts, vampires, ghouls, and humanity itself, who were forced to carve out civilization on an untamed Continent already inhabited by elder races like elves and dwarves.

To survive the deadly beasts that infested the wilderness, mages and monarchs subjected children to mutagenic alchemy, grueling combat training, and the agonizing Trial of the Grasses. Those rare few who survived emerged as witchers: sterile, mutated, yellow-eyed mercenaries built for one sole purpose—hunting monsters for coin.

The Witcher's Creed & The Lesser Evil

"Evil is evil, Stregobor. Lesser, greater, middling, it's all the same... If I'm to choose between one evil and another, then I prefer not to choose at all."

Though common folk revile them as emotionless mutants, witchers live by a code largely invented by themselves to navigate hostile courts and superstitious villages. Geralt's philosophy repeatedly challenges the illusion of neutrality, showing that even in a world governed by blood and political cynicism, empathy and personal bonds remain the only true anchors.
